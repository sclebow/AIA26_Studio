Under Hood

How the Frontend & Backend work together

Three parts: what the designer sees, the engine that runs everything, and the AI brain that thinks.
FRONTEND
What the designer sees & does
💬Talk to it in plain wordsType design intent — the copilot reads it and answers your questions.
🏙️See it in 3D, on your real siteThe building & site (with setbacks) update live as you edit.
🗂️Browse everything you've madeSite → wings → saved & optimized versions, all in one tree.
📊Live design healthSun, view & noise scores update the moment geometry changes.
BACKEND
The engine — memory, tools, history
🧠MemoryRemembers your site, every shape & edit for the whole session.
🛠️ToolsGenerate geometry, apply setbacks, optimize, validate — on demand.
🕘History & decisionsEvery choice is recorded & explainable — replayable, not a black box.
📡Live updatesStreams the agent's reasoning to the screen as it happens.
AI BRAIN
Reasons, decides, acts
🔁Think → act → checkPlans the next step, runs a tool, checks the result, repeats.
✨Understands design languageTurns "open toward the park" into the right geometry move.
✅Validates its own workCatches invalid geometry and fixes it — no broken buildings.
You talk → the engine remembers & runs the tools → the AI brain reasons, acts, and checks itself → you see it in 3D, with every decision explained.
