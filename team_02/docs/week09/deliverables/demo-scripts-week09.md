# Sensi / Final review · demo voiceover scripts

Recorded by **Emilie**, played over the 3 demo clips, interleaved per act so the jury sees the tool early. Paced ~150-160 wpm. These match the deck clip `<aside>` notes.

---

**Clip 1 · Demo / Onboard · Emilie**

Sensi starts by getting to know you. A few quick questions: your name, your role, the story of the space you're shaping. You toggle the senses that matter most. You say who lives here, and the one thing you won't compromise on. Then it learns your eye. You type an aesthetic in your own words, Sensi gives you rounds of images, and you keep what feels right. Three passes, and a moodboard takes shape. Then it compiles your answers and your choices into a single sensory persona: your weights, your priorities, in its words. That persona is the lens for everything that follows.

**Clip 2 · Demo / Shape · Emilie**

Now the real work. We load an actual floor plan, layout 201, and Sensi draws it as clean CAD linework: walls, doors, windows, furniture. We hit analyze, and in a few seconds every room is scored across all six senses, shown as a six-petal rose: the bigger the petal, the better that sense. Right away a conflict surfaces, a sense flagged below your personal bar. Before we fix it, look at what the agent can do: this menu is its full vocabulary, organized by sense. Nine edit tools, add a window, change a wall, hang curtains, add ventilation, swap a material, plus ways to see the plan differently. So we just say, in plain language, change the kitchen walls to cork. It plans the edit, applies it, and Focus Pull takes over: the whole plan dims and only what changed lights up, right where it is. Hover, and you see the before, the after, and which scores moved. The room re-scores, and the suggestion that flagged it crosses itself off. Open the checkpoint graph, and every sense is a strand you can watch weave as you edit. And the galaxy shows the whole project as one map, hover any thread and the Narrator explains it in plain words.

**Clip 3 · Demo / Report · Emilie**

This is the Report, and notice what greets you first: your persona, and the moodboard you built at the very start. The output opens by remembering your input. We expand a room. It reveals in stages: the scores, then the reasoning, then the room itself, rendered. A first-person image of how that space feels to be in: this room, with its real comfort scores turned into light, material, and air. And for a room we edited, we can wipe between before and after: same room, same angle, only the change moving, so you see the improvement, not just read it. That's the whole loop, closed. You told Sensi who you are; it read your plan, scored how it feels, and showed you the trade-offs, and every room you walk through here, you've already shaped, without lifting a pencil.
