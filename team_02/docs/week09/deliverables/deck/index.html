THE RIPPLE

One move ripples through every sense.

Each standard scores a sense in its own silo, so the second cost, and the way it feeds back on the first, stays invisible until someone lives with it. Could a designer catch this while it is still a plan?

R3 · coupled    Multi-sensory thermal comfort review, 2025    cross-modal: one sense moderates another, additive, synergistic, or compensatory
[Maria · ~30s] Take one ordinary move, a bigger window. The daylight gets better, that is the upgrade you see. But the same glass is a thinner sound barrier, so noise gets in, and it leaks heat, so the room is colder in winter. Those are the costs you do not see. And here is the ripple: the noise it let in even dims the daylight you gained, one sense moderating another. One move, and the whole room shifts. So we asked, could a designer catch this while it is still a plan?

[Maria · ~24s] Here is why we start with you. This is one plan, read through two people. Slide the lens to a child, who minds noise, and the loud living room lights up as the problem. Slide it to a grandmother, who minds warmth, and the cold bedroom does instead. The plan never changes; who is standing in it decides what you feel. That is why Sensi opens by learning who you are.
